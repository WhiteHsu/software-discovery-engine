## Owner-run procedure

1. Record the session metadata and plan evidence. If the target edition is unavailable, record `not-entitled` and leave its product execution pending.
2. Paste the **exact before text** for a case. Wait for completed analysis and current counters; do not record stale suggestions from the prior input.
3. Capture accessible feedback: highlight/issue wording, offered suggestion and rule/category if exposed. Record a paid/quota prompt as a block; do not invent the identity of locked rules.
4. For a reproducible manual-edit comparison, replace the input with the **manual reference after text**, then capture the completed recheck. If also testing a vendor suggestion, run it as a separate clearly labelled observation and record its exact resulting text.
5. Record screenshot/file references plus date/settings/version. Include only the sample text and relevant UI; credentials and account identifiers are unnecessary.
6. Repeat for the five cases. Record no-feedback, service errors and blocked sessions honestly. None automatically qualifies or rejects the entire product.
