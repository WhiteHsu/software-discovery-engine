## Proposed workflow and evidence boundary

Select a short personal English prose passage, inspect style feedback, manually decide whether to revise, then recheck in the same editor. For a future comparison, use samples at most 2,000 characters including spaces as a conservative review bound. This does not resolve LanguageTool's conflicting product limits.

Selected research editions: Hemingway Editor 3 Classic desktop, hosted LanguageTool Basic with limited Picky Mode, and ProWritingAid **Premium lifetime** account. Premium Pro is separately documented, but its higher optional quotas are unnecessary for the proposed line-editing scope.

Basic style checking is supported at a category level. A complete common rule set is **not yet verified** across these exact editions. Do not turn the proposed scope into a qualifying alternative claim. Documentary descriptions are not hands-on results or evidence of equal accuracy.
