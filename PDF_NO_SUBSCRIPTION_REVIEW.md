Existing-text editing and annotation must be separate capabilities, not substitutes for page organization. Confirm each required operation for each edition before a later draft graph can qualify. The route title can remain PDF Editors Without a Subscription only with a visible explanation that the shared comparison concerns page operations.

Exclude promises of complete Acrobat parity, enterprise document systems, signature validity, secure redaction, OCR accuracy and preservation of every font/layout. No native applications or export fidelity were tested. Two products documenting text editing do not establish three text-editing alternatives; do not broaden scope merely to reach three candidates.

Adobe desktop documentation establishes the reference overlap: [extract pages](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/desktop/edit-documents/organize-pages/extract-pages.html) and [rotate pages](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/desktop/edit-documents/organize-pages/rotate-pages.html). These support a proposed bounded editorial relationship, not a tested replacement guarantee.
