## Hemingway free web: bounded hands-on result

[H1] explicitly offers free scoring and difficult-sentence highlights, separately advertising Plus AI simplification. [H2] was exercised anonymously in Edit mode, without login, Plus trial enrollment or paid action.

Original two-sentence sample: the passive and complex prose from PR5.44/46. The editor showed three passive uses, one very hard sentence and grade 12. After a manual active/shorter rewrite, it showed zero passive uses, zero hard/very hard sentences and grade 7.
