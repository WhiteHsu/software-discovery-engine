## Why a different shared operation is not approved

The positive control was labelled a grammar issue in the UI. Current [Hemingway highlighted-issue documentation](https://hemingwayapp.com/help/docs/highlighted-issues) separates grammar/spelling/punctuation and AI fixes as Plus premium features. Those capabilities cannot be assigned to Classic. This documentation does not establish full Classic parity with Basic's correction.

Hemingway's [readability documentation](https://hemingwayapp.com/help/docs/readability) describes scores and flags but marks target adjustment premium. It does not resolve Classic purchasing terms. General LanguageTool statistics or an official community rule catalogue likewise cannot establish the same exact hosted-Basic operation. Catalogue names do not supply tier allocation.

Accordingly, do not redefine the workflow as "any writing feedback" or count the grammar control merely to reach three candidates. A defensible replacement requires an explicitly reviewed third candidate or separate edition, paired anchor evidence and exact operations. No candidate or edition is automatically substituted.
